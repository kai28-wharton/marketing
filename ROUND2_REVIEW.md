## New team strategy and segment focus

The new **Customer segmentation decision** page (`/#segmentation`) separates
growth fit from existing-equity protection. Keep S3 as the fit anchor, not a
guaranteed win; compare S5 with S6 as the principal scale alternatives. Experience
intensity orders the debated four S5 > S6 > S4 > S2, while existing aggregate CLV
orders S2 > S6 > S5 > S4. The page evaluates the strongest case, financial/capacity
risk, current named rivals and evidence required to promote each segment. Its
discussion-group selector generates a one-line offer implication and observed
portfolio totals; no allocation or strategy adoption is automatic.

The team has shifted to **Experience leadership plus a very competitive Brand**.
Low Price and Frictionless are now fair-value safeguards rather than the second
differentiator. “Experience top 1” means **rank #1 among actual retailers**, not
merely Getchell’s strongest quadrant. Neither deck mandates a single segment: the
only “one” rule is **one quadrant** (“Be BEST in one quadrant, and use that
advantage to leverage a 2nd quadrant,” Intro p17; “Move on just ONE dimension …
One-trick ponies don’t survive,” MV p8), while segments are addressed in the
plural (Intro p9, p24). The recommendation is therefore a **tiered group**:

**Why Segment 3 is the core.** It is the only segment whose explicit profile is
Brand / Experience, and it has the highest raw Brand intensity (0.30) with the
second-highest shopping-experience intensity (0.40). Its limitation is scale:
10.87% of market consumers and 11.61% of reported segment CLV. Strongest fit
does not mean largest pool.

**Why Segment 5 is conditional.** It is larger, has higher aggregate CLV, and
has the strongest shopping-experience intensity (0.43). But its documented
Frictionless / Experience profile makes broad acquisition premature while
Frictionless is **1.90 below** the CSV floor. Protect existing Segment 5
customers now; require contribution, retention and convenience-parity evidence
before scaling acquisition.

Segment 3 + 5 represent **26.84%** of market consumers, **30.46%** of reported
aggregate segment CLV, and **32.49%** of our current segment revenue. These are
different denominators: market consumers are not our buyers, aggregate CLV is
not per-customer value or profit, and revenue mix is not penetration. No
segment-specific profit, acquisition cost or elasticity is supplied.

## Why Experience + Brand is the best strategy (MarketValue_Slides)

The choice is a documented strategy judgment, not a validated forecast. The
MarketValue deck states the mechanisms; it does not calibrate a spending-to-score
coefficient.

|Documented rule|Slide|Verdict|Our position|
|---|---|---|---|
|Two differentiators with parity on the other two|MV p8|Pass|Experience + Brand differentiate; Low Price (+1.00) and Frictionless sit at CSV fair value|
|Match strategy to the segment’s actual needs|MV p9 step 1; Intro pp7–9|Pass|Segment 3 is the only segment whose top two raw needs are Shopping Experience (0.40) and Brand (0.30)|

|Charge a fair amount for the value delivered|MV p9 step 3|Conditional|Frictionless is −1.90 below its 5.0 floor; leaving a reduce-pain quadrant under fair value risks the customers the strategy needs|
|Extend into a second credible quadrant|MV p8; MV p9 step 4|Conditional|Experience is credible (#2, 5.8); Brand is the gap—#4 at 4.1 vs BESTCO 4.9|
|Do not neglect non-target customers|MV p9 step 5|Pass|Defend Segments 2 and 6; keep credible fair-value offers|
|Convince the market you can repeat it|MV p9 step 6; MV pp3–4|Conditional|A durable brand lowers perceived risk/WACC; two-quadrant coherence supports a shorter horizon T|
|Target the CLV / brand-adjusted valuation route|MV pp6, 11|Pass|Expensed brand and customer assets still earn full credit through the CLV route|
|Anticipate competitive moves|Intro pp8–9|Risk|Segment 3’s rival ZEPHARA is the Experience leader (6.5); treat S3 as a contested fit core and test before scaling|

**Why not the other pairings.** *Low Price + Experience* fits Segment 6 but
markup cuts act immediately with no persistence (MV p12), compress free cash
flow, and BESTCO already owns that segment. *Frictionless + Experience* matches
Segment 5’s strongest Experience pull (0.43) but starts from our weakest quadrant
(3.1 vs CADABRA 7.5); online capacity is partly capitalized, adding debt and WACC
pressure from a weak position. Brand + Experience ranks best on fit, capital
efficiency and use of the CLV/brand valuation route.

**Competitors already targeting each segment (Intro p8).** Segment 3 → ZEPHARA;
Segment 5 → ZEPHARA; Segment 4 → AIM / ZEPHARA / KTBENNEY; Segment 2 → AIM /
PRICEMART; Segment 6 → BESTCO; Segment 1 → CADABRA. Competition is a first-order
input: highest fit in Segment 3 comes with the strongest incumbent.
