TEXTBOOK EXPLANATION

Breadth and depth are different choices

Breadth: variety across product lines

A broad retailer covers more categories and shopping missions. Offering coffee, tea, home goods and personal care is broader than selling only coffee. Breadth may support one-stop shopping and appeal, but can dilute positioning and increase operational complexity.

Depth: variety within a product line

A deep coffee assortment offers more brands, roasts, formats and sizes within coffee. Depth is not automatically stock units per SKU. It can improve fit, but may create choice overload, slow-moving items and markdowns depending on stocking policy.

Your four indices

  • Lower-cost breadth: variety across lower-cost product lines.
  • Lower-cost depth: choice within those lines.
  • Higher-cost breadth: variety across higher-cost product lines.
  • Higher-cost depth: choice within those lines.

65 does not establish 65% of the budget, 65 categories or 65 SKUs. The settings do not have to add to 100. Multiplying breadth by depth does not establish SKU count. The simulator’s exact definitions still need verification.
